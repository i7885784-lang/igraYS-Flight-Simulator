Adaptation by this project: YFT reader, normalization to the Su-34 envelope,
own polygon colors/insignia, separated surfaces, fixed armored glazing,
source tyre recovery from fragment children, mirrored/reused source tyre for
right/tandem main wheels, sampled gear and control mechanisms, true internal
apertures and closed curved doors, own support links and fixed-axis AB visuals.
Baked GTA weapons are omitted; native game weapons use adapted source pylons
and own tip launch brackets. Other existing aircraft/assets retain their notices.

Small source fork corner adjustments, axial tyre adaptation and oleo stroke
are recorded in model-stats.json. Entrance hatch/ladder are not part of this test.
