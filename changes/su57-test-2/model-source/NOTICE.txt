Adaptation: coordinate conversion, normalized dimensions, blue-white polygon
camouflage, YSFlight DNM control/animation hierarchy, gear bays and mechanisms,
cockpit interior, intake liners, nozzle seals, markings and collider.
This adapted Su-57 mesh and corresponding conversion sources are supplied
under GPL-3.0. No warranty. Other aircraft and YSFlight retain their licenses.
No FlightGear executable or FlightGear flight systems are included.

Rebuild model data (Python 3 with numpy and scipy):
  python build_model.py
Outputs: runtime/aircraft/su57.dnm, su57_coarse.dnm, su57.dat,
su57coll.srf and su57cockpit.srf relative to this source directory.
The converter requires no Blender or AC3D editor.
