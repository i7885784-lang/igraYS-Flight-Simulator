YSFlight v8 Airshow — 2026-10-19
Functional checks used a separate Win64 GL1 build of the same source under Wine
10/software OpenGL/Xvfb 1920x1080. They did not run the packaged 32-bit EXEs.

PASSED
- Native new-flight UI: four selected wingmen; distinct per-slot palette/RGB
  values survived saving. The Russian Airshow tab and F10 panel were visually
  inspected; supply, sync, auto reheat, burst length/interval and sensitivity
  controls are present. Mission button is absent from the main screen.
- Formation descent: player and four F18 followers descended from 200 m to
  ~35.396 m over flat terrain, stayed airborne and held column positions. Final
  four slot errors were ~0.034 m in the final 61-second check. Native record and
  JSON evidence included. This is an easy steady descent, not a universal bound.
- Pull-out: four followers initially at 150 m with -12 degree pitch all survived
  a 41-second run. Their minimum recorded altitudes were 109.87–118.32 m; after
  the protective action they continued following the leader. Native record kept.
- Formation turn: eight regular followers completed a physical 180-degree turn
  and returned to INPOSITION. See final JSON/log for actual slot error, duration
  and membership; unrelated aircraft are excluded from squad counts.
  Final native record: 107.204 s, heading -179.891 deg, all eight INPOSITION,
  maximum slot error 54.837 m. The 90-second scripted phase was followed by
  a physical Pause-key resume for ~15 seconds to let the last member settle.
- Crossing paths: two fighters exchanged left/right formation positions and
  remained alive; recorded minimum centre distance was 17.145 m. Avoidance is
  gradual with physical response time, not a guaranteed separation boundary.
- Helicopters: UH60 leader and two AH64 followers remained alive for 46.557 s.
  Followers' maximum absolute banks were 8.917 / 9.004 degrees. No helicopter
  reported afterburner activation. This does not test all helicopter manoeuvres.
- Physical F10 opened the show menu on a legacy control profile. All five
  aircraft dispensed native finite flares: 500 -> 484. A second burst for selected
  wingman only changed its count to 468 while the others remained at 484.
  Native smoke flags and distinct RGB colours were enabled for every member.
- Manual player's reheat at full throttle: afterburner=1, throttle=1.0; external
  screenshot shows both exhaust plumes on the player's F18. No helicopter flame
  is added; existing jet/non-reheat/AMX visual policy remains in force.
- Physical camera drag accumulated heading 6.720 radians (>360 degrees). Wheel
  reduced distance from 100 to 56.743 m; menu changed focus to a wingman. Drag
  cursor restoration and suppression of fire/weapon-cycle input are implemented.
- Native YFS effect record contains MAXNMFLR/LOADWEPN/SMOKEOIL/SMOKECOL commands;
  replay was opened and actual recorded smoke was visually inspected at 9.80 s.
  Replay setup restores generators/colours without refilling flare ammunition.
- Both x86 GL1/GL2 builds completed. Packaging separately verifies PE32/i386,
  only system DLL imports, stripped code equality, original assets/config and
  prior-v8/original-2018 EXE backups, paired translations, ZIP paths/CRC/manifest.

DEFECTS FOUND DURING VALIDATION AND CORRECTED
- Applying a new pursuit controller to every situation degraded distant recovery;
  native guidance is retained at safe heights, with local protective overrides.
- Native inverted catch-up and repeated aggressive pursuit of a low slot could
  create a second dangerous dive. Low slots use gentle altitude guidance across
  the approach; recovery reserve includes roll and vertical-velocity response.
- Smoke oil is a WEIGHT in YSFlight. Final show equipment uses stock aerobatic
  100 kg, not a time-like quantity. Correct inventory mass was retested.
- Original F10 was GHOSTVIEW, not free. Migration replaces that standard binding,
  respects other custom bindings and explicit AIRSHOWKEYSET changes; wheel
  centre also opens the panel. Old control IDs remain unchanged.
- Show equipment changed directly in properties was missing from native record
  commands. Explicit load/oil commands now preserve recorded equipment/colours.

LIMITATIONS
Ready-made x86 executables must be checked on the user's Windows 10 32-bit.
GL2, audio and performance on that machine were not runtime-tested here.
No absolute crash-prevention guarantee: all terrain, buildings, wind, aircraft,
engine failures and extreme manoeuvre combinations are not exhaustively tested.
No ground-taxi formation, automatic group landing or invulnerability is added.
Large flare/smoke displays use native rendering resources; use smaller bursts
and groups on slow computers. Only the named functional scenarios were run.
